The normative design and mathematical contract are in
[`environment_v2_spec.md`](environment_v2_spec.md). The implementation review and
V1.4-to-V2 decisions are in
[`environment_v2_design_review.md`](environment_v2_design_review.md).

records only and do not define the active runtime.
